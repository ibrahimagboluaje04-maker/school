How to get started

A simple guide for families.

Choosing a school is an important decision. We encourage families to explore our website, speak with our admissions team and arrange a visit to experience the Cedar Grove community.

1

Make an enquiry

Contact our admissions team with your child's year group and any questions you may have.

2

Arrange a school visit

Visit the campus, meet members of our team and learn more about daily school life.

3

Understand the application

Our team will explain the relevant application forms, documents and next steps for your child's year group.

4

Speak with our team

We will guide you through any further assessment, meeting or placement information that may apply.
