Who we are

A school with a heart for learning.

Get to know the people, purpose and values that make Cedar Grove Academy a place to learn and belong.

Our story

Built on a simple belief: every child can flourish.

Cedar Grove Academy was founded with a clear purpose: to offer children an education that combines academic growth with strong values, creativity and care. Over the years, our school community has grown through the commitment of families, staff and learners who believe that education can open doors.

Today, we continue to create an environment where students are encouraged to think independently, treat others with respect and take pride in their progress.
