A school that knows its pupils

A place where every child is known.

We want pupils to leave each day having learned something new, tried something difficult and treated someone well. Our teachers take time to understand how each child learns and help them make steady progress.

  • Teachers who take an interest in each child’s progress.
  • Clear lessons in literacy, numeracy, science and the arts.
  • Clubs, sports and group activities that build confidence.
